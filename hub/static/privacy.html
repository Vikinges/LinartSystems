Privacy Policy

LSC LED Doc mobile app & Linart Systems backend · Last updated: 24 June 2026

This policy explains what data the LSC LED Doc app and the Linart Systems backend (lsc-led.de) collect, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and how you can have it deleted. The app is a professional field-engineering tool used to create and manage service / installation / maintenance reports.

1. What we collect

  • Account data — your username / email and a hashed password used to sign in.
  • Report contents — free-form notes, project numbers, customer and site names, equipment details and other fields you enter into reports.
  • Photos you attach to a report as work evidence.
  • Signatures captured to sign off a report.
  • Coarse location — only when you use the in-app site-address picker, to help fill the site address. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Basic technical data — app version and device type, sent with requests so we can keep the service working.

2. How we use it

Data is used solely to provide the reporting service: to authenticate you, generate the PDF reports you create, let you and authorised colleagues retrieve them, and obtain client signatures. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Where it is stored

All data is stored on servers located in the European Union. Connections use HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never in plain text.

4. How long we keep it

Account and report data are retained for as long as the account is active, so that past reports remain available to you and your organisation. When an account is deleted (see below), the account record is removed after a 7-day grace period.

5. Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account directly inside the app: Profile → Delete my account. This calls our POST /api/account/delete-request endpoint, immediately signs you out, and schedules your account for removal after a 7-day grace period (logging back in within that window cancels the deletion). You can also request deletion or ask any privacy question via the contact below.
